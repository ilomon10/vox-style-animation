# Wanted: gaps in the shared catalog

Contributions welcome (public domain or CC0; sound may be CC BY). Workflow: `public/assets/library/README.md`.

## Images
- **Energy and environment:** power lines and transmission towers, coal and oil power plants, wind and solar (US
  government sources: DOE, NREL photos are often not PD, so check), smog, floods, wildfires (USFS), glaciers (USGS).
- **Places outside the US and Europe:** street life, markets, transport and government buildings from Asia, Africa,
  Latin America and Oceania (Commons PD-old / CC0; national archives that release CC0 or PD).
- **Generic people:** crowds, workers, families and children from a wider range of places and decades, without names
  attached.
- **Objects for cut-outs:** telephones, radios, televisions, typewriters, cameras, light bulbs, money and coins (US coins
  by the US Mint are PD), clocks, globes, books, newspapers, shopping bags, cars, trains, ships and aircraft shot on
  plain grounds.
- **Documents:** pre-1931 newspaper front pages (Chronicling America), US patent drawings, typed government memos,
  census forms, tickets, stamps (check stamp rules country by country).
- **Diagrams:** labelled technical cutaways, anatomy plates (Gray's Anatomy 1918), simple charts from old textbooks.

## Maps
- Historical maps of East, South and Southeast Asia, South America and Oceania (the catalog has the US, Europe, the
  Roman Empire, southern Africa, Arabia and world maps); city plans other than London; shipping-route and airline maps.
- Natural Earth rivers, lakes, populated places, admin-1 states and provinces (data entries and rendered styles).

## Sound and picture
- **SFX:** paper tears, typewriter keys and bell, camera shutter, film projector loop, stamp thud, pen scribble,
  marker squeak, crowd murmur, ticking clock (CC0 recordings preferred).
- **Music:** public-domain or CC0 beds (FreePD is gone; Musopen and Commons PD recordings are good leads).
- **Footage:** PD newsreels and government films (NASA, NARA, NOAA), city street scenes from more countries.

## Derived assets
- AI cut-outs of people and objects already in the catalog (`add.py cutout`).
