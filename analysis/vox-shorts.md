# Vox Shorts (9:16): canvas, hook, captions and timing — `vox_short_*`
Three official Vox Shorts, uploaded 12–14 Nov 2026 (private reference: `reference/vox-sample/shorts/`). All positions are in 1080×1920 px; the 720p files are scaled ×1.5 (±1.5 px). Frame numbers are global at each file's fps.

## 0. Inputs and reliability
- **Viewed:** 15/15 sheets, 19 native stills, 3 strips (all in `analysis/stills/vox_shorts/`).
- **Measured (under the lock):** a yellow-box tracker over all 7,095 frames; 772 karaoke events aligned to 783 whisper words, plus 26 audible onsets after pauses; 86 caption pages; backing photometry on flat paper; font fits; a scale match of the creatine checklist against the long-form; mid/side levels and a pitch split of the voices.
- **Shot detector:** correct for healthcare (18/18) and Mississippi (13/13; its "soft transitions" are a handheld whip inside the AP clip). Creatine: f70 is a cut inside the TikTok and f2185 a panel addition; it missed a jump cut (f507) and an in-place swap (f1916); its f299 "dissolve" fails a blend test. **Every editorial transition is a hard cut.**
- **Distrust:** whisper starts on connected speech run 2–4 f early; small yellow boxes lose chroma at 720p (`#F8F009` for `#FDF100`); music and SFX are inferred from levels (I could not listen).

**Chunking (86 pages):** 2-line pages hold a median of 11 words (p10–p90 8–14), 1-line pages 5 (1–7); a page stays up for a median of 3.3 s. 37 of 40 sentence ends end a page; the other breaks fall where two lines are full (46 % of pages end mid-clause, 11 % at a comma). One-word answers get their own page ("Yes." "Maybe." "No."). A page appears 1 f before its first highlight and replaces the previous one directly (median gap 1 f); it holds through pauses and runs across cuts. The first page is up by f0–2.

**Karaoke highlight:**
- **Box:** `#FDF100` (small boxes read `#F8F009`), radius ≈ 3 px; line-box height (46–48 px, 3 px beyond ascenders and descenders) × word ink + 7–8 px per side. The word inside turns `#000000`.
- **Order:** every word in turn (772 events for 783 whisper words; the misses are short words and edited text); hyphenated words as one. The box holds until the next word; the last word stays lit until the page changes.
- **Pop:** the box, not the text, scales about its centre, by frame after onset: 0.96, 1.03, 1.05, 1.04, 1.01, 1.00 (same in all three Shorts, n = 256).
- **Timing:** −2 f median (range −5…0) before the audible onset of 26 post-pause words; +1.7 / +2.6 / +2.2 f median after whisper starts (IQR +1…+4, n = 730), because whisper's starts run early.

## 7. Spec for our framework
All values are design px on a 1080×1920 canvas at 24 fps. Use our own marks and tokens, never Vox's.

**(a) `ShortCaptions`**
1. **Type:** Roboto 500, 45 px (cap 32, x-height 23), letter-spacing −0.02 em, line-height 50.5 px, `#FFFFFF`, no stroke or shadow.
   - Sentence case, with punctuation and numerals kept.
   - Text comes from the corrected script; timing comes from the words file.
2. **Pages:** ≤ 2 lines, line ink ≤ 735 px, centred on x = 540.
   - Fill greedily, and always end a page at a sentence end.
   - A sentence tail or a one-word answer gets its own 1-line page.
   - Expect ≈ 11 words on a 2-line page, ≈ 5 on a 1-line page, and ≈ 3.3 s per page.
3. **Backing:** one rectangle per page = the union of the line boxes + 10 px left/right and + 9 px top/bottom (≈ 108 / 60 px tall). `#000000` at 26 % opacity, radius 0.
4. **Placement:**
   - On full-frame shots, the block centre sits at y = 1688 (baselines 1674/1725; a single line at 1700).
   - On two-up shots, it sits on the seam at y = 1010 (baselines 997/1047; a single line at 1022).
   - The block is fixed to the canvas, and pages run across cuts.
5. **Karaoke box:** the line-box height (48 px: ascender −3, descender +3) by the word ink + 7.5 px per side.
   - Fill `packaging.lemon` (Vox measured `#FDF100`), radius 3 px.
   - Draw it between the backing and the text; the active word turns `#000000`.
6. **Box timing:** on at `round(start × fps)` for each word, never later than the audible onset (Vox: +2 f vs whisper starts, −2 f vs the onset).
   - The box holds until the next word.
   - The last word holds until its page is replaced.
7. **Pop on every word:** the box scales about its centre 0.96 → 1.03 → 1.05 → 1.04 → 1.01 → 1.00 over frames 0–5. No fade; the text does not move.
8. **Page swap:** a page comes in 1 f before its first word's box and hard-swaps the previous page (no fades, no empty frames inside a phrase). The first page is up by frame 0–2.

**(b) `ShortHook`**
1. **Box:** flat `packaging.lemon`, radius 0, centred on x = 540, 864 px wide (observed 863–1014). Its height is the text + 36 px top and bottom (observed 33–39; 75–81 once).
2. **Text:** Roboto 900, ALL CAPS, `#000000`, 60 px (cap 42), line-height 1.0–1.1 em, centred.
   - 2–3 lines, each ≤ 870 px, with ≥ 70 px side padding.
3. **Copy:** the title, or the title as a yes/no question that the Short answers (≤ 8 words).
4. **Position:** box centre y = 1420 (observed 1395–1448).
   - Use the upper slot at y = 1110 when the lower slot covers the subject.
   - Keep the box bottom ≤ 1585.
5. **Timing:** fully on at frame 0, with no entrance animation; it is the first frame autoplay shows.
   - It holds through the cold open and leaves on a hard picture cut: the first cut after the opening sentence, 3–10 s in (observed 3.1, 7.0, 10.3 s).
   - Captions run under it; no name label appears while it is up.

**(c) Reframing to 9:16**
1. **Always full-bleed:** no letterbox, pillarbox, blurred fill or windows.
2. **Landscape footage:** crop at ≥ full source height (×1.78 for 16:9), placed on the subject.
   - Edge text may be cut.
   - An optional slow move: ≈ 6 % zoom or ≈ 100 px of drift per shot.
   - **Photos:** the same crop plus a ≈ 6 % pull-out over 4–5 s.
3. **Talking heads:** crop 16:9 webcams full-bleed with the face centred and the eye line at 0.30–0.50 H. Use vertical self-shot video as is. Jump cuts are allowed.
4. **Name label,** on the first appearance only:
   - **Box:** `packaging.lemon`, radius 0, width fixed per video (600–864), height ≈ 200–225, ≈ 44 px padding above the name cap and below the role (observed 27–61).
   - **Type:** name in a heavy sans, cap 39 (≈ Roboto 900 54 px); role in an italic serif, cap 30 (≈ Source Serif 4 Italic 45 px), 1–2 lines at a 62 px pitch.
   - **Slot:** centre y 1450 when the eyes are ≤ 0.35 H; otherwise centre y 300.
   - **Timing:** hard on at the speaker's first cut-in (≤ 1.2 s after it), hard off at the next cut; 2.7–6 s on screen.
5. **Conversations:** a two-up stack.
   - Top 1080×1010 = the active speaker; bottom 1080×910 = the listener.
   - Seam at y = 1010, with no gap; the captions sit on the seam.
   - Mix ≈ ⅓ two-up, ⅓ single speaker, ⅓ b-roll.
6. **16:9 graphics:** scale the whole composition ×0.63 (→ 1210×680) and centre it (x −65…1145, y 619…1300) on its own ground, extended to the full canvas. Keep the long-form timing.
   - Documents may run larger and overflow the width.
   - Lifted strips run edge to edge.
7. **Paired images:** stack two near-square cards, ≈ 800–840 px wide, at y ≈ 48 and ≈ 963 (≈ 60 px gap). The second card enters on its keyword; an optional ≈ 9 f rack focus on the first.
8. **Source credits:**
   - Vertical social clips run full-bleed with a top-left tag: icon + handle, white ≈ 21 px cap, on a translucent dark box inset 6 px.
   - Footage credits are white ≈ 20 px text, rotated 90° CCW, at x ≈ 50–120, mid-height.

**(d) Pacing**
1. **Length:** 80–110 s of content (observed 79–105 s), then a 2–5 s end card.
2. **Speech:** from frame 0, at 2.5–3.0 words/s overall (150–180 wpm) and 2.8–3.4 w/s articulation, with 16–20 pauses ≥ 0.3 s.
3. **Cuts:** hard cuts only, 7–13 per minute (mean ≈ 10). Median shot 3–6.5 s; no shot over ≈ 15 s (observed max 15.4 s) unless the picture changes inside it.
4. **Hook:** 3–10 s from frame 0 (rule b5). The first name label follows it.
5. **End:**
   - Hard cut from the last word to the end card, 3–6 f after the word ends.
   - End card: full-frame brand colour. The first element comes 3–8 f after the cut, the logo builds in 5 f, and the tagline follows ≈ 1.1 s after the logo.
   - Add an optional "watch the full video" CTA on cut-downs. No loop matching.
6. **Sound:** dry dialogue, or a bed 7–18 dB under the voice. The bed is unducked and stops dead at the end card.
